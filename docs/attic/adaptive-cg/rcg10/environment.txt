Addendum (2026-08-15): Human decisions and the re-run matrix
------------------------------------------------------------
Three Human decisions (Anders Bergman) after the first audit pass:

  1. The legacy GNU make path is not a supported build for current GPU builds.
     CMake is the supported build system; the legacy gate is withdrawn.
  2. Finite temperature is allowed at the atomistic level; coarse blocks stay
     at T=0.  Accepted as the model, not rejected as a gap.
  3. The independent adversarial review was performed by the Human owner and
     is what opened RCG-09A, RCG-09B, RCG-09C and RCG-09D.

Decision 2 was given fixture backing in this session:

  tests/coarse_graining/e2e/finite_temperature_mixed
    static_mixed at temp 10.0 with tseed 4711 -> fine=1 interface=4 coarse=1.
    run_production_e2e.py requires its trajectory checksum to separate from
    the T=0 static_mixed reference (48.051222 vs 48.000000).

  Negative control (executed): neutralizing the thermal field collapses the
  warm trajectory onto the cold one and the assertion fails with
    "mixed-resolution thermal field did not move the fine atoms:
     warm=48.0 cold=48.0"

  fixture_dependencies.all_e2e_cases() did not previously list
  FINITE_TEMPERATURE_CASE, so that fixture's inputs were never covered by the
  PKG-TRACKED-E2E audit.  Both finite-temperature cases are now registered.
